## What only JoJo has (the idea comes from this list)
1. The address: 3 W 47th — the block the whole industry buys from.
2. The age: started at 15, "America's youngest". The brand is a person, not a house.
3. Custom pieces that are characters: a money-bag with an R face, a bee on an M. Jewellery with a personality.
4. Black satin: every piece film is shot on crushed black fabric. The ground of the site.
5. Rose gold under white stones: the pieces lean rose/pink, not yellow.
6. The clients' own words on the store: Lil Baby, Lil Mabu, 22Gz.
7. Watches as a second trade: source, sell, part-exchange.
8. 50% deposit, wire/Zelle/Cash App: a real process, not a cart.

## Idea sentence
**On this page, the black satin the pieces are filmed on becomes the page itself; the jewellery is the only light.**
Competitor test: Icebox, Johnny Dang and IF & Co all run white or grey catalog grounds with gold accents; none shoots on satin or puts a 20-year-old's name on the door. The sentence does not fit them.

**Live thing (V1):** pick metal and width on a Cuban and watch the price and gram weight update, with the length guide against a body silhouette.
**Live thing (V2):** a 3D chain you drag, with the four real custom pieces as full-screen chapters.
