HOW IT WORKS
A simple process on getting paid for your timepiece
FILL IN THE FORM
            Fill out the above form, and we'll check out the 
            watches you have for sale. If we are interested, we 
            can talk about shipping or coming to the branch.
VISIT/SHIP FOR INSPECTION
            Send us your watches by mail or stop by the branch 
         so we can inspect them.
PRODUCT INSPECTION
            We will examine and evaluate your watches to make 
            sure they are authentic and of the appropriate quality, 
            including any accompanied paperwork.
